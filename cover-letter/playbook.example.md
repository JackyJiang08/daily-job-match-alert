# Cover Letter Playbook

A starting point. `npm run setup` copies this file to `private/cover-letter/playbook.md`; edit that copy
(or replace it in the hub under Settings > Cover Letters). Every letter prompt includes the whole playbook,
so keep it to rules and facts you want in every letter.

## Voice

- First person, plain and specific. One idea per sentence.
- No em or en dashes as punctuation; use commas, periods, or colons.
- No slang, no filler ("passionate", "excited to leverage", "I believe I would be a great fit").
- Prefer the posting's concrete needs over general praise of the company.

## Who I Am

- Degree, school, and graduation month exactly as the resume states them.
- GPA, if you want it mentioned, written as "with a 3.8 GPA".
- Work authorization, if it matters for the roles you apply to.

{{AVAILABILITY}}

## Evidence Library

List the projects and results a letter may cite. Each line: what you did, the tool, and one number that
appears word for word on your resume. Letters never use a number that is not here or on the resume.

- Example: Built a weekly reporting pipeline in SQL and Python that cut a manual review from a day to an hour.
- Example: Trained a classification model on 40,000 support tickets; precision rose from 0.71 to 0.83.

## Rules for Repeated Metrics

- Cite each number at most once per letter.
- Never combine numbers from two different projects in one claim.

## Closing

- Two sentences: a thank-you, then one forward-looking sentence about the team or the start date.
